# Pro Agreement Summary

## About this document

This is a summary of the main terms that apply when you use BiTadilat as a pro. Full agreement text: [FULL PRO AGREEMENT — to be drafted by a lawyer]. The Terms of Use and the Payment and Secure Hold Terms also apply to pros.

## 1. Independent work

The pro is not an employee, agent or partner of BiTadilat; the pro works independently. Complying with tax, insurance, workplace and trade rules, and issuing the required invoice, is the pro's responsibility.

## 2. Application and verification

- The application asks for business name, bio, experience, categories and districts served, and documents (ID, trade certificate, tax document, criminal record).
- An admin reviews the documents; the outcome can be approval, a request for more documents, or rejection. You cannot send offers until approved. If rejected you see the reason and can resubmit documents.
- Documents must be accurate and current. A forged document leads to account closure.

## 3. Offers

- Offers state labour and materials separately; amounts must be greater than zero. The total includes VAT.
- The arrival window and the workmanship warranty (0, 3, 6 or 12 months) are yours to set; an offer is valid for at most 72 hours.
- You can have only one pending offer per request at a time. You must honour an accepted offer; asking for extra payment outside the platform is prohibited.

## 4. Carrying out the job

After the customer accepts the offer and pays, the full address is visible to you. You confirm the appointment or propose a new window; you mark the stage in the app when you arrive, start and finish. Cancellation is possible only up to the on-site stage and is recorded on your record.

## 5. Service fee and payment

- The platform service fee is 10% and is deducted from the amount paid out to you.
- The money is held in the secure hold; it is paid out on customer confirmation, or 48 hours after you mark the job finished if there is no open complaint. Before a payout the payment provider is asked whether the payment has any refund; if it cannot be reached the payout is postponed and retried automatically, and if a refund shows the payout is not made and the record is reviewed by hand.
- In a complaint the admin decision applies: in a full refund nothing is paid to you; in 50/50 half the amount is paid to you; no service fee in either. If the complaint is dismissed the amount is paid to you and the 10% service fee applies.

## 6. Conduct and sanctions

You must treat customers respectfully, do the work as offered and not move payments off the platform. Violations can lead to a warning, suspension or closure; the system records warning and cancellation counts.

You can close your account at any time; ongoing jobs and amounts in the secure hold are settled under the rules. [TERMINATION AND NOTICE PERIODS — lawyer to confirm]
